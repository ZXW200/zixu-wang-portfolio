Publication

Auditing what medical AI learns.

PRCV 2026 · First author

How Far Can a Single Lead Go? A Construct-Validity Audit of ICD-Based ECG Benchmarks on 54,357 MIMIC-IV Patients

Zixu Wang, Richard Jiang

Benchmarked six model families on 54,357 MIMIC-IV patients using patient-level splits. The audit combined no-ECG baselines, age-stratified evaluation, label sensitivity and zero-shot transfer to PTB-XL.

A no-ECG baseline reached AUC 0.816, compared with 0.800 for the best ECG model on the composite ICD target. Within age strata 50–79, ECG models exceeded the no-ECG baseline by 0.05–0.07 AUC, showing how aggregate and within-stratum comparisons differ.

MSc dissertation · 2026 · Sole researcher

Region-Level Explainability on PD-DBS Facial Images

Developed and evaluated an eight-region Anatomical Evidence Vector pipeline for auditing pre-/post-DBS image classification within the Explainable Face2Gene Diagnosis project.

  • 2,343 grayscale 32×32 images; NumPy MLP and compact CNN
  • Test AUROC 0.9824; 0.9337 on the similarity-filtered subset (maximum train-cosine 0.98; n = 310)
  • Four regional contrasts retained after confidence matching and FDR correction
  • Right cheek/zygomatic ranked first by balanced accuracy in 10 of 11 atlas variants
